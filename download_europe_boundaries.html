Europe Country Boundaries

Download the European Commission GISCO country-boundary GeoJSON dataset and save it in your website repository as europe-boundaries.geojson.

Open original dataset
Tap “Download GeoJSON file”. If direct downloading is blocked by your browser, use “Open original dataset” and then use your browser’s download/share options.

Source: European Commission GISCO. The download button fetches the dataset and creates a local file. This requires internet access and may be blocked by browser CORS rules; the original dataset link is provided as a fallback.
